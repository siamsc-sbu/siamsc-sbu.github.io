About the chapter

We’re Stony Brook’s student chapter of the Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics (SIAM).

Who can join?

We welcome undergraduate and graduate students from any major. If you’re curious about how people use math to solve problems, you’re welcome at our meetings and events.

Email the chapter to ask about upcoming meetings, suggest an event, or find out how to get involved.
